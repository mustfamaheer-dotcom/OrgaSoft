## 📱 How to Add Your Business Email to Gmail

Follow these steps to connect your `orga4soft.com` email to your Gmail app on your mobile phone or computer:

### **On Mobile (Android/iOS)**
1. Open the **Gmail App**.
2. Tap your profile picture in the top right corner and select **"Add another account"**.
3. Choose **"Other"** from the list of email providers.
4. Enter your full business email address (e.g., `admin@orga4soft.com`) and tap **Next**.
5. Select **Personal (IMAP)**.
6. Enter your email password and tap **Next**.
7. **Incoming Server Settings:**
   - **Server:** `mail.orga4soft.com`
   - **Port:** `993`
   - **Security Type:** `SSL/TLS`
8. **Outgoing Server Settings (SMTP):**
   - **Server:** `mail.orga4soft.com`
   - **Port:** `465`
   - **Security Type:** `SSL/TLS`
9. Tap **Next** and complete the setup.

### **On Computer (Gmail Web)**
1. Open Gmail in your browser and log in to your personal/regular Google account.
2. Click the **Gear icon (Settings)** ⚙️ -> **See all settings**.
3. Go to the **Accounts and Import** tab.
4. In the "Check mail from other accounts" section, click **Add a mail account**.
5. Enter your business email address and click **Next**.
6. Select **Import emails from my other account (POP3)**.
7. Enter the following:
   - **Username:** Your full email address.
   - **Password:** Your email password.
   - **POP Server:** `mail.orga4soft.com`
   - **Port:** `995`
   - Check the box: *"Always use a secure connection (SSL) when retrieving mail"*.
8. Click **Add Account**.
9. When asked if you want to be able to send mail as this address, choose **Yes** and follow the SMTP setup (SMTP Server: `mail.orga4soft.com`, Port: `465`, SSL connection).
